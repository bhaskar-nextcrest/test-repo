# WebMCP Integration Guide

URL: https://aptness.ai/

## MCP Tools
1. **ai_agent_builder** — For creating and managing custom AI agents.
2. **workflow_designer** — For visually designing and automating workflows.
3. **natural_language_interface** — Handles chat-powered interactions and understanding.
4. **application_connector** — Manages connections to various external applications.
5. **data_transformation_engine** — Processes and transforms data for AI and workflows.

## Integrations
1. **CRM Systems** (REST): Connects to customer relationship management platforms.
2. **Productivity Suites** (REST): Integrates with office and collaboration tools.
3. **Communication Platforms** (REST): Links with messaging and team collaboration apps.
4. **Database APIs** (REST): Connects to various types of databases.
5. **Custom REST APIs** (REST): Allows integration with any RESTful service.

## Endpoints
- `GET /agents` — Retrieve a list of AI agents.
- `POST /workflows` — Create a new automation workflow.
- `GET /integrations` — List all configured application integrations.
- `POST /chat` — Send messages to AI agents for interaction.
- `PUT /data_sources` — Update details of a connected data source.

## Setup
1. **Create Account & Onboarding**: Register and complete initial platform setup.
2. **Connect Applications**: Integrate necessary third-party services and data sources.
3. **Build First AI Agent**: Design and configure your initial custom AI agent.
4. **Define Workflow**: Automate a business process using your AI agent and connected apps.
5. **Test & Deploy**: Validate your setup and put your automation into production.
